Lets it sign in and enter your CVV by itself. Encrypted with your passphrase (AES-256) and kept only in this Chrome profile. You unlock it once each time Chrome starts. One-time codes texted or emailed by a store can't be automated: it alerts you for those.

Buying rules

Speed

Under 3 seconds per store tends to get you rate-limited, and a blocked watcher is slower than a patient one. It backs off automatically if a store pushes back.

Alerts

Install the free ntfy app on your phone, tap +, and subscribe to this exact topic. Anyone who knows the name can read it, so keep it random.

Optional. Pushover's emergency alerts keep ringing until you acknowledge them, even overnight.
